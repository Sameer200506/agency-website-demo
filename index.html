04 how we pricetap to expand

A one-week diagnostic: we audit your current build, interview your team and deliver a prioritised plan with estimates. It's €4,800 and most of it gets credited back if we continue.

Frequently. Roughly half our work is alongside existing product teams — we slot into your rituals and leave behind code your engineers enjoy owning. We don't do knowledge-hoarding.

Thirty days of support is included. After that most clients move to a light monthly plan covering monitoring, small experiments and a standing design hour.

TypeScript everywhere. Next.js or Astro for the front end, Postgres for data, and whatever CMS your editors will genuinely use. We don't migrate you for sport.

05 process

Five moves,
no mystery.

Every project runs the same spine. It keeps the work legible and keeps surprises in the open where we can discuss them.

avg. timeline9–14 weeks
  1. 01

    Discover

    Stakeholder interviews, analytics teardown, technical audit of whatever exists today. We write down what we learn before we design anything.

    Week 1Written brief
  2. 02

    Shape

    Two to three directions explored properly — not three grey boxes. We prototype the risky parts first so the unknowns surface early.

    Week 2–3Prototypes
  3. 03

    Build

    Fortnightly increments in a repo you own from day one. Staging on every push, tests where they earn their keep, and a live preview link in Slack.

    Week 4–11Fortnightly demos
  4. 04

    Launch

    Redirect maps, analytics, consent, Core Web Vitals gates and a documented rollback. Launch nights are calm because we've rehearsed them.

    Week 12Handover
  5. 05

    Evolve

    Thirty days of support, then optional monthly work. We measure what changed and tell you honestly whether the next idea is worth it.
